## Model assumptions

The calculator assumes a balanced sinusoidal system and uses line-to-line voltage with line current. Its apparent-power relationship is `|S| = √3 VLL IL`. Lagging reactive power is positive and leading reactive power is negative.

The model does not account for unbalance, harmonics, transients, losses, efficiency, demand, protection, metering conventions, or electrical-code requirements.

</section>

## Try these theory checks

- Keep voltage and current fixed, then lower the power factor and compare apparent power with real power.
- Switch between lagging and leading and identify which calculated values change sign.
- Set the power factor to one and explain why reactive power and the power-factor angle become zero.

These are original study prompts written for this site.
