Useful electrical knowledge is easier to build together when every contribution carries its context. Use this guide for page discussions, corrections, resource suggestions, and future GitHub contributions.

:::caution[Discussion is not field direction]
Community contributions are unreviewed. Do not use a comment or proposed edit as authorization for electrical work. Follow your employer, qualified supervision, applicable law, and the requirements in force where the work occurs.
:::

## Ask a question others can answer

Include:

- the concept or task you are trying to understand;
- what you currently think is happening;
- the exact point where your reasoning breaks down;
- a diagram, units, or terminology when relevant; and
- the province or territory and code edition if the question depends on them.

Avoid posting recalled exam content, personal information, employer-confidential material, or unsafe requests for remote approval of field work.

## Suggest a source others can verify

Provide the source title, publisher, edition or date, direct link, and the claim it supports. Prefer an official authority for regulatory and certification facts, and manufacturer documentation for product-specific information.

If a source conflicts with another source, include both and explain the difference you noticed. A conflict may come from date, jurisdiction, scope, or a genuine error.

## Propose a correction

A strong correction includes:

1. the page and section;
2. the wording or idea that may be wrong;
3. the proposed replacement;
4. the source and applicable scope; and
5. why the change helps a learner.

Comments, when enabled, are hosted through GitHub Discussions and are labelled as community contributions. They do not become reviewed learning material automatically.

Before contributing, read the [publishing standards](/publishing-method/).
